## 💡 **Tips for Writing**

1. **Be honest about limitations** - Readers respect transparency
2. **Include specific numbers** - "34% faster" not "much faster"
3. **Show, don't just tell** - Code examples, scenarios, diagrams
4. **Credit others** - Mention Hindsight, OpenAI, tools used
5. **Link to resources** - GitHub, docs, demo video
6. **Proofread carefully** - Typos hurt credibility
7. **Keep paragraphs short** - Easier to read online
8. **Use subheadings** - Helps skimmers find what they need
